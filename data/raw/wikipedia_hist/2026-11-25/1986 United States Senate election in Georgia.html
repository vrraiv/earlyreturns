The 1986 United States Senate election in Georgia was held on November 4, 1986. Republican incumbent Mack Mattingly ran for re-election to a second term but was narrowly defeated by U.S. representative Wyche Fowler.[1]

This was the last time a Democrat was elected to this seat for a full term until 2022 and the second of three consecutive elections for this seat in which the incumbent was defeated.
