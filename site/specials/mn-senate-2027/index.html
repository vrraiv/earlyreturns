Minnesota Senate special election

Minnesota, class 1: a special election in November 2027. Amy Klobuchar (DFL) leaves the seat in January 2027.

Klobuchar was elected governor of Minnesota in 2026. An appointee holds the seat until the special election.

No forecast yet. The numbers appear once the inputs below are in; until then this page holds the race and its dates.

What the forecast waits on

  • The certified 2026 House vote, national and Minnesota's, for the state's lean and the national environment (usually certified in December).
  • The appointee, and whether they run (the incumbency term).
  • The nominees, after the primaries.
  • The exact election date, from Minnesota's Secretary of State.

Key dates

How this is made

The forecast is the single-race path for a Senate special: the state's lean in the last federal election before the date, the off-cycle national environment, and twice the incumbency term; polls, when there are any, are weighed against it by precision. The error is a t distribution with an SD of 18 points. That SD was measured on the 13 past Senate specials the path was checked on, so it was chosen from the same races, not tested on new ones.

The steps and the rules are in the design note on special elections, sections 1, 2 and 5. Probabilities appear only from a branch whose design was settled before this race.
